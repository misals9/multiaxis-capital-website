The Pipeline

From Raw Data to Portfolio Decisions

Our proprietary pipeline automates every step with institutional-grade rigor. Each component is independently validated and monitored.

01

Data Acquisition & Processing

We ingest data from institutional-grade financial data vendors, processing millions of data points across Indian listed equities. Our infrastructure handles corporate actions, survivorship bias, and data quality issues automatically through continuous validation and cross-source reconciliation.

02

Feature Engineering

Domain-informed feature construction that translates financial and economic theory into quantitative signals. Our feature library spans fundamental, technical, and alternative data dimensions — each feature is grounded in financial intuition before being validated empirically.

03

Model Development

Machine learning models trained with walk-forward validation, ensuring no look-ahead bias contaminates results. We employ rigorous out-of-sample testing and statistical significance checks before any model reaches production. Every model must pass multiple layers of validation.

Data Foundation

Institutional-Grade Data Infrastructure

Financial AI is only as good as its data. We've invested heavily in infrastructure that guarantees temporal correctness and eliminates common biases.

Point-in-Time Alignment

All data is timestamped and joined as-of the decision date, preventing look-ahead bias that plagues many quantitative strategies. We reconstruct the information set exactly as it existed at each historical point.

Survivorship-Bias Free

Our universe includes delisted and merged companies, ensuring backtests reflect realistic historical conditions. This is critical for honest performance assessment in the Indian market context.

Automated Quality Assurance

Continuous data validation, outlier detection, and cross-source reconciliation catch errors before they propagate into model training or portfolio decisions.

Deep Historical Coverage

15+ years of historical data across Indian equities enables robust statistical inference across multiple market cycles, regimes, and economic environments.

Research Methodology

Academic Rigor, Production Delivery

We apply the standards of peer-reviewed academic research to every model we build. No shortcuts, no overfitting, no data snooping.

Walk-Forward Validation

Models are trained and tested on strictly non-overlapping time windows, simulating real-time deployment conditions. No future information leaks into training data at any stage.

Statistical Significance Testing

We test for statistical significance using bootstrap methods and account for multiple hypothesis testing — not just point estimates. This guards against strategies that look good by chance alone.

Factor Attribution

Every strategy is decomposed against known risk factors to isolate genuine alpha from systematic risk premia. We know exactly what drives our returns and why.

Academic Baselines

We replicate established asset pricing frameworks (multi-factor models, momentum strategies) as baselines, ensuring our methods genuinely improve on the state of the art rather than rediscovering known effects.
